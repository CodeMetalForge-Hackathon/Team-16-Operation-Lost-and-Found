Map key: what the symbols mean

Where to look

Search heat
Where the person is most likely to be. Redder means more likely. It fades where the pod has already searched.
Trail hot spot
A likely spot on or near the planned trail. #1 is the most likely.
Off-trail hot spot
A likely spot away from the trail, such as a drainage, stream or ridge.
Faded ring = already searched
The pod has flown over this hot spot.
Search next / searching now
The 500 m box the pod is flying to or sweeping: the area with the most probability left. Once it's searched, a new box is picked.
Stream
Lost hikers often walk downhill along water, so streams are searched closely.
Planned trail
The route the missing person was on.
Hiker was here (simulation)
Shown only after a simulated search ends: where the hiker was really hidden.

Places

Summit
Mountain peak. Only the big ones show when zoomed out; zoom in to see all.
Trailhead / parking
Where hikes start. In "click a point" mode, click one to use it as the last known point.
Landmark
Hut, ravine, waterfall or other named place.

The pod (drone)

Pod
Where the pod is right now.
Pod track
Where the pod has flown. Its camera covers about 60 m on each side.
Terrain fix
The pod confirmed its position without GPS by matching its lidar to the height map.

What the pod found

Camera detection
The camera spotted a person or an unnatural color, such as an orange jacket.
Beacon: hiker confirmed
The camera saw a person. The pod dropped a radio beacon on them so the ground team can walk straight there.
Beacon: possible sighting
The camera saw something unusual (e.g. an orange patch). The pod marks it with one of its ~10 beacons and keeps searching; a ground team checks it.
Radio: our beacon
The pod hears the dropped beacon. A higher dB value means it is closer.
Radio: unknown transmitter
Another signal on the same band, logged for the team.
